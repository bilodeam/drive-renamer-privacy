Privacy Policy

Tab Stash stores your saved tab groups and session data exclusively inside your browser's local storage (chrome.storage.local). We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any browsing history, tab URLs, analytics, or personal data on external servers.

Permissions Used

  • tabs: Used solely when you click Stash or Restore to read open tab titles/URLs in the active window and reopen them when requested.
  • storage: Used solely to keep your stashed tab groups saved locally on your device.
